Our mission

Make financial information easier to understand.

Managing personal finances can become complicated when information is scattered across different accounts, spreadsheets and applications.

Finora brings important financial information into one organized experience, helping users see their income, expenses, budgets, savings and financial trends more clearly.

The goal is simple: provide a clean and understandable interface that makes everyday financial planning feel less overwhelming.
