Important demo notice

Finora is currently a static portfolio/demo website. It does not operate as a bank, financial institution, payment service or investment platform.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes the general approach to information and privacy for the Finora website.

Because the current Finora experience is a static front-end demonstration, many production-level data collection and account features are not active.

2. Information we may receive

Depending on how the website is used, information may be provided voluntarily through interactive features such as the contact form.

Contact information

If a future production version receives a message, it may process information such as your name, email address, subject and message content.

Demo data

Financial figures shown throughout the current Finora interface are demonstration data and are not intended to represent real financial accounts.

3. How information is used

Information provided through a future production version may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to questions or support requests.
  • Understanding product feedback.
  • Improving the user experience.
  • Maintaining website functionality and security.

Information should not be used for purposes unrelated to the reason it was collected without appropriate notice or legal basis.

4. Data storage

The current Finora demo does not provide a real user account system or financial database.

Some browser-based demonstration features may use local browser storage for functionality. Such data remains within the user's browser unless a future version explicitly connects the application to a backend service.

5. Third-party services

A production version of Finora may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, email delivery, authentication or other technical functionality.

When third-party services are introduced, their respective privacy policies and data practices should also be reviewed.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information handled by a production application.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid submitting sensitive financial credentials through ordinary contact forms.

7. Children's privacy

Finora is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. A future production service should apply appropriate safeguards and requirements where applicable.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Finora website or its features change.

Any future production version should publish the updated policy and its effective date so users can understand what has changed.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can use the Finora contact page.
